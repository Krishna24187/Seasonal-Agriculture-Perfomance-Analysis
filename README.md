# 🌾 Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

An empirical data analytics project evaluating crop yield, resource efficiency, environmental drivers, and financial performance across 4,000 agricultural farm records in 8 Indian states across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons. Developed as part of the **VOIS AICTE Industry Internship Program (2026–2027)**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Agricultural productivity and farm profitability are heavily impacted by seasonal variations, irrigation choices, soil health, and input allocation. This project processes and analyzes a multi-variable agricultural dataset to identify key productivity drivers and provide data-backed recommendations for sustainable farming.

### Key Objectives:
- Evaluate seasonal crop yields ($t/ha$) and profit margins across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid cycles.
- Measure water-use efficiency ($t/1000m^3$) across Flood, Drip, and Sprinkler irrigation systems.
- Analyze the economic impact of soil pH, NPK fertilizer usage, and pest risk.
- Deliver actionable insights for farmers, policy makers, and agri-tech stakeholders.

---

## 💡 Key Analytical Findings

1. **Seasonal Dominance:** **Kharif** delivers the highest average productivity (**5.64 Tonnes/Ha**) and maximum profit, leveraging natural monsoon rainfall.
2. **Irrigation Efficiency:** Precision **Drip irrigation** ($6.27\ t/1000m^3$) provides nearly **$2\times$ higher water efficiency** compared to traditional Flood irrigation ($3.44\ t/1000m^3$).
3. **Cost Drivers:** High atmospheric humidity accelerates pest/disease exposure, significantly elevating plant protection expenses per hectare.
4. **Soil Health Alignment:** Optimal crop yields concentrate within neutral soil pH ranges (6.5–7.5), optimizing NPK nutrient absorption.
